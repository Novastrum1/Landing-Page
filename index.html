ONE CLEAR STRATEGY

Four checks before every trade.

The Novastrum Core Strategy reads the market top-down. If any check fails, the bot waits. No trade is also a decision.

  1. 1Bullish

    Daily bias

    The daily chart sets direction. Higher highs: buys only. Lower lows: sells only.

  2. 2Watching

    4H liquidity sweep

    Price runs past a recent 4-hour high or low, takes the stops resting there, and turns back.

  3. 3Waiting

    1H order block

    The bot marks the last opposing candle before the move and waits for a return to it.

  4. 4Waiting

    15-minute entry

    A shift on the 15-minute chart confirms it. The bot enters with its stop already set.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Email hello@novastrum.com.

What is Novastrum?

An AI trading assistant that runs one clear strategy on forex, gold and crypto, inside your own broker or exchange account, with limits you set and a reason for every trade.

Can Novastrum withdraw or hold my money?

No. Access is trade-only. Novastrum can place and close trades, but it can never deposit, withdraw or move your money.

What’s the difference between paper and live?

Paper mode runs the same strategy on real prices without risking any money, so you can see how it trades. Live mode places real trades in your own account. You start in paper, and going live is always your choice.

Which brokers and exchanges will work?

Brokers that support MetaTrader 5, and crypto exchanges that offer API keys with withdrawals switched off. Keys that allow withdrawals are rejected.

How is risk managed?

Every trade is sized before it’s placed and has its stop loss set at entry. By default that’s 1% per trade and 3% a day, with a 2% hard cap per trade. News Shield pauses new trades around high-impact events, and the emergency stop halts new trades instantly.

Does Novastrum guarantee returns?

No. No automated system can promise a profit. Trading carries real risk, including losing money, and paper results don’t guarantee live results.

When will I get access?

We invite people from the waitlist in small groups, starting with paper trading. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when your invite is ready.
